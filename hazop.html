HAZOP-X: AI-Driven Process Safety

Modernizing Operational Risk Assessment

HAZOP-X is an AI-driven safety infrastructure designed to automate and simplify process hazard analysis (PHA). By continuously evaluating plant piping diagrams and operational bounds, it verifies potential vulnerabilities, optimizing industrial safety protocols.

Risk Assessment Pipeline

3

Pilot Project Notice

100% Free & Open Evaluation

HAZOP-X was our pilot project to test the capabilities of Large Language Models. We tested different LLMs and chose Google Gemini to launch our pipeline.

Our engineering team designed and deployed the orchestration and semantic reasoning framework that extracts and builds complex piping logic from P&IDs. While Google Gemini provides the foundational large language model capabilities, our safety-contextualized prompt architecture and constraint engine ensure structured, rigorous, and industry-compliant hazard identification.
