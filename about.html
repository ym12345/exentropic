The Minds Fueling Innovation

Exentropic combines veteran process design expertise with modern machine learning research to deliver robust industrial AI suites.

Dr. Nandu Karajgikar

Co-Founder & CEO

Over 45 years of experience in the hydrocarbon industry specializing in kinetics and reactor modeling. Dr. Karajgikar holds BS, MS, and PhD degrees in Chemical Engineering and has executed more than 100 simulation projects across 4 continents, including distillation units, refining processes, petrochemicals, LNG liquefaction/re-gasification, and combined cycle power plants.

Dr. Yogesh Makwana

Co-Founder & CTO

Machine learning expert with extensive experience building industrial AI systems. Dr. Makwana holds a Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering from the University of Manchester and B.Tech/M.Tech degrees from IIT Bombay. Formerly Co-Founder & CTO of Syvum Technologies and R&D Engineer for the HYSYS suite at Hyprotech (now AspenTech).

AI and Machine Learning Division

The AI and Machine Learning Division is responsible for building, training, and testing our deep neural networks. They specialize in integrating thermodynamic equations directly into deep learning layers, managing high-throughput plant data feeds, and keeping simulation errors to a minimum.

Process Engineering Division

The Process Engineering Division is responsible for first-principles verification. Fusing decades of design expertise in refinery piping layouts, reactor design constraints, and safety guidelines, they review all digital twin case configurations and audit HAZOP-X safety reports.
